# R-AI-cing Nexus V7 Design Baseline

This document is the documentation-only baseline for the Phase 0 design-rescue branch. It preserves the original V7 cinematic benchmark as the minimum visual bar while keeping all demos synthetic, rights-aware, and non-wagering.

## Source pack

The protected source pack is `NEXUS_V7_EVERY_STEP_QUALITY_GATE_HANDOFF.zip`, supplied outside the repository. Its SHA-256 is recorded in `PHASE0_STATUS.md`. `V7_REFERENCE_MANIFEST.json` records the individual reference paths, dimensions, tiers, roles, and SHA-256 values.

The pack contains:

- nine immutable V7 golden references, including the original board and Tactical Track crops;
- supporting functional studies for the fourteen planned workspaces;
- high-fidelity concept references, which are creative benchmarks rather than built screens;
- review records documenting the V14/V15 visual regression;
- the visual gate, evidence templates, rights/design-state guidance, and backlog specifications.

No image assets are copied into the application in this phase. The manifest is an integrity and provenance record, not a licence grant.

## Non-negotiable visual contract

- Every visual change must meet or exceed the original V7 cinematic benchmark. Flat V14/V15 racing diagrams are rejected as visual treatments.
- Tactical Track must preserve cinematic racecourse depth, a visible inside rail, individually placeable runners, natural two- to three-abreast settling groups, and premium navy/gold information hierarchy.
- Thoroughbred, harness, and greyhound mechanics remain distinct. A Tactical Track view represents early settling approximately 0–400 m from the start and must not claim precisely validated predicted positions.
- All demonstrations are labelled synthetic/illustrative. Do not invent live or official odds, source permissions, model performance, or confidence.
- Betting, staking, order placement, trading, provider activation, provider credentials, Auth0 changes, Render deployment, and edits to The Long Straight are outside this phase.

## Workspace retention

The design programme retains all fourteen workspaces: Command Centre; Meetings & Race Discovery; Tactical Track; Runner Intelligence; Runner Comparison; Model Laboratory; Markets & Value; Evidence & Provenance; Research Assistant; Notebook; Watchlists; Alerts; Reports; and Settings/navigation.

## Evidence and approval

For any changed UI, the eventual evidence package must include real browser screenshots at 1440×900, 768×1024, and 390×844; a pinned V7 reference; the same synthetic fixture across views; rights/design-state declarations; and a distinct human reviewer plus owner decision. Automated checks can validate file integrity and declared evidence, but cannot provide artistic approval, legal clearance, race-domain validation, or owner sign-off.

The current Phase 0 state is HOLD for Tactical Track implementation: no renderer candidate, screenshots, independent review, or owner decision has yet been produced.

## Next reviewable increment

The next implementation increment is an isolated Tactical Track design laboratory behind a feature flag or standalone route. It must compare a layered 2D/sprite candidate with a practical GPU/3D challenger where feasible, use one synthetic fixture, and stop at REVISE/HOLD if the result is visually weaker than V7.
