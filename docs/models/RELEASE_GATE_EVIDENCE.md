# Release-gate evidence contract

The synthetic release-gate report is the join point for local engineering
evidence and decisions that CI cannot make. It is generated by
`scripts/generate_release_gate_fixture.py` and verified by
`app.release_gate.verify_release_gate_report`.

The current fixture is deliberately `LOCAL_SYNTHETIC_ONLY_HOLD`:

- automated validation and local evidence are passing;
- visual, accessibility, model-assurance and owner decisions are pending;
- approved OIDC and real-source rights are explicit external dependencies;
- `activation_authority` and `promotion_authority` are permanently `NONE`;
- wagering and external connectivity must remain disabled.

The S4 packet at `fixtures/synthetic/s4_assurance_packet_v1.json` maps the
machine evidence to the named visual, accessibility, racing-domain, model,
security, OIDC, and provider-rights gates. It is a preparation artifact, not
an approval record.

The report is hashed so stale or edited gate states fail verification. A
passing local pilot state would still be a scoped, owner-approved synthetic
pilot; it would not authorize live providers, production deployment, model
promotion, trading, staking or wagering.
