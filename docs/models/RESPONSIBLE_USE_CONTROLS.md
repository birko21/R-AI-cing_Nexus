# Journal summaries, annotation export and responsible use

RIL-045 keeps personal selections/PASS records separate from model forecasts and paper financial simulations. Summaries count selection types and forecast references without importing model outputs or paper results.

Permitted export is `ANNOTATIONS_ONLY`: selection rationale, field revision, decision time, and correction notes may be exported when allowed. Raw prices and forecast payloads are explicitly excluded, and export is denied when the capability is not permitted.

Session controls support owner pause, quiet hours, and a maximum session duration. When a control blocks continuation, the reason is explicit and reflection-oriented. Copy validation rejects chase-loss and urgency cues such as “chase losses,” “win it back,” “last chance,” “act now,” and “urgent.” The fixture is synthetic and offline.
