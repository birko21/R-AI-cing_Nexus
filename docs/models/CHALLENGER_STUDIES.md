# Challenger studies and promotion gate

RIL-055 compares a named challenger against `B0_UNIFORM`, `B1_FORM_HEURISTIC` and `MARKET_BASELINE` on the same preregistered chronological holdout. Each score stores sample size and a confidence interval. The study and fixture are synthetic-only evidence; they do not validate a real-world model.

Promotion is `HOLD` unless the challenger beats every baseline and both independent assurance and explicit owner approval are recorded. No automated result can make a challenger champion without those controls.
